Use in the first few weeks

This sequence follows the confirmed topics: place value, comparing and rounding, addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, including decimals. It is a proposed practice sequence, not a verified reconstruction of a particular week-by-week plan or an official publisher resource.

Suggested focusStationsListen for
Week 1Place value; Compare & roundValue of a digit; zeros; ten-times relationships; decimal size; midpoint reasoning.
Week 2Addition; SubtractionLike units; regrouping preserves value; exchanging across zeros; decimal alignment.
Week 3Multiplication; DivisionDistributive reasoning; equal groups; fractional quotients; inverse checks.

Use 10–15 minutes after concrete teaching, or as a paired station. Ask pupils to estimate first, use the model, then explain a fresh example without it. Adjust timing to the class.

Colour choices

Students see only RED, ORANGE, GREEN and BLUE. Every colour is available immediately; assign the appropriate colour separately for each station.

ColourTask range
REDWhole numbers, two- and three-digit regrouping, multiplication by a single digit and exact whole-number quotients.
ORANGELarger whole numbers, tenths, initial decimal calculations and some two-digit factors/divisors.
GREENHundredths, decimal comparison, regrouping, decimal factors and fractional quotients.
BLUEThousandths, internal zeros, rounding across a whole-number boundary and more demanding decimal products/divisions.

There are four fixed tasks per colour in every station: 96 total. Pupils can revisit any task. Colours describe the chosen task set, not a label for a child.

Student routine

  1. Choose your colour and station.
  2. Read the task and estimate the result.
  3. Build or change the model. Use Hint, Undo or Reset when needed.
  4. Enter an answer where asked. Check and use the feedback.
  5. Explain your method to a partner; move to another task.

Use a decimal point and no commas in answer boxes. At narrow widths the place-value bench scrolls horizontally. Students can use the large tap buttons; no dragging is required.

Model and assessment notes

  • Each counter represents its column value. Equal-looking counters in different columns do not have equal value. Ten smaller units exchange for one next-larger unit.
  • Addition begins with the two addends already combined; pupils normalise the columns and interpret the result. Subtraction begins with the minuend and requires removing the subtrahend after necessary exchanges.
  • Multiplication uses a schematic area partition. The rectangles are labelled accurately but are not drawn to scale. Accept different correct partitioning methods in discussion.
  • Division measures groups of the divisor’s size. A 0.1-group step uses one tenth of the divisor. Fractional groups are numbers of groups, not remainders.
  • Rounding tasks use positive numbers and the conventional half-up classroom rule. No negative numbers, fractions, order of operations or remainder interpretation is assessed here.
  • A stamp confirms a completed model and answer. Hints can reveal a worked method, so stamps are practice evidence, not proof of independent mastery. Ask for reasoning and transfer.

Access and privacy

Scan the QR code or open the game link. No login or installation is required. The app collects no student name or email. Progress is stored only in that browser on that device; it does not sync. Download a record before changing devices or clearing browser data. On a shared device, use My record → Clear this device between pupils after saving any needed work.

Test the public link on one school iPad before the lesson to check school network access. Physical iPad and Safari testing has not been completed.
